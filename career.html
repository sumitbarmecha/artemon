Engineering

Sr. / Jr. FEA Engineer

Structural analysis and simulation role focused on aerospace component integrity, optimisation and validation.

Pune, India Full-time B.E. Mechanical / Aerospace
View Details Hide Details

Job Overview

The FEA Engineer will be responsible for structural analysis and simulation of aerospace components to ensure design integrity and performance under operational conditions.

Key Responsibilities

  • Perform static, dynamic and thermal simulations
  • Validate designs and prepare detailed technical reports
  • Work closely with CAD and design teams
  • Optimise structures for performance and weight reduction

Required Skills

  • Ansys, Abaqus, Nastran
  • Strong understanding of solid mechanics
  • Experience with FEM mesh generation
  • Knowledge of aerospace material properties

Qualification

B.E. / B.Tech in Mechanical or Aerospace Engineering. Prior internship or project experience in simulation is a plus.

Simulation

Sr. CFD Engineer

Computational fluid dynamics role focused on aerodynamic, thermal and fluid flow optimisation for UAV platforms.

Pune, India Full-time B.E. Mechanical / Aerospace
View Details Hide Details

Job Overview

Responsible for performing computational fluid dynamics simulations for aerodynamic and thermal applications across Artemon's UAV platform range.

Key Responsibilities

  • Conduct CFD analysis for fluid flow and heat transfer
  • Perform meshing, simulation and result validation
  • Optimise aerodynamic surfaces for efficiency
  • Support propulsion and structural teams with simulation data

Required Skills

  • Ansys Fluent, OpenFOAM, STAR-CCM+
  • Thermodynamics and fluid mechanics
  • Turbulence modelling experience
  • Post-processing and technical reporting

Qualification

B.E. / B.Tech in Mechanical or Aerospace Engineering. Experience with external aerodynamics simulation preferred.

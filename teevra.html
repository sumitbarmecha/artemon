Platform Overview

Built for Extended-Range Tactical Missions

TEEVRA MK-I is a high-endurance loitering UAV engineered for extended-range tactical missions, precision targeting and advanced aerial surveillance. Designed for modern defense operations, the platform delivers enhanced payload capacity, superior flight stability and reliable performance across complex operational environments.

With advanced guidance systems and autonomous capabilities, TEEVRA MK-I enables real-time situational awareness and precision engagement. The system supports flexible deployment through canister-based and pneumatic launchers, ensuring rapid readiness across ground and maritime missions.

Why TEEVRA MK-I

TEEVRA MK-I offers a balance of endurance, payload capacity and precision, making it a powerful loitering UAV platform for extended tactical missions. Its ability to operate in GNSS-denied environments, execute autonomous operations and support swarm deployment ensures high mission effectiveness in demanding defense scenarios.
